fog/california

A live loop of fog and low cloud over the entire state — coastal marine layer, Central Valley tule fog, and everything between — seen from 22,000 miles up by NOAA's GOES-18 (GOES-West) satellite, Pacific Southwest sector. New frames arrive about every 5 minutes; this page refreshes itself automatically.

GeoColor shows natural color by day and blends in infrared at night, so low fog stays visible around the clock. IR 3.9 µm is the shortwave-infrared band forecasters use to pick out fog and stratus in the dark.
